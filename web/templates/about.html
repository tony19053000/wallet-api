About Pocketa

Building the next generation of social payments for digital India.

Our Mission

Pocketa was founded with a clear goal: to make peer-to-peer payments as effortless and friendly as texting. Whether you're sharing a meal with roommates, settling a trip bill, or sending a birthday gift, Pocketa eliminates the friction of traditional banking apps.

Key Milestones

  • 2024: Founded in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
  • 2025: Reached 100,000 active users and ₹50 Crore monthly transaction volume.
  • 2026: Expanded features with instant bill splitting, money requests, and automated ledger reconciliation.
