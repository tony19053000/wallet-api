Frequently Asked Articles

How do I top up my Pocketa wallet balance?

Go to Home > Add Money, select an amount (minimum ₹1.00, maximum ₹50,000.00), choose UPI, Card, or Netbanking, and confirm. Funds are credited instantly with zero charges.

What are the daily transfer limits for Basic vs Full KYC?

Basic KYC users have a daily send limit of ₹10,000.00. Upgrading to Full KYC via Aadhaar & PAN increases your daily send limit to ₹1,00,000.00.

How does withdrawing money to a bank account work?

Navigate to Withdraw, select your primary Indian bank account, and enter the withdrawal amount. A flat fee of ₹2.00 is charged, and funds are transferred via IMPS immediately.

How do I split a bill with multiple friends?

Go to Request & Split, enter participant @handles, type the total amount, and add a note. Pocketa calculates each person's exact share and sends payment requests automatically.

Can I change my @handle after creating my account?

Yes, you can edit your handle from your Profile page, provided the chosen handle is available and not reserved by another user.

Are wallet top-ups or peer-to-peer transfers charged any fees?

No! All wallet top-ups, transfers to friends by @handle, and bill requests are 100% free of charge.

What happens if a money request is declined?

If the designated payer declines a request, its status changes to 'declined'. No money is debited or moved from either wallet.

How do I download my monthly account statement CSV?

Open Activity, filter by date range if desired, and click 'Download statement (CSV)' at the top right to download a complete ledger file.
